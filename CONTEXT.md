# Resume Tailoring

Personal resume tailoring for individual job applications.

## Language

**Master resume**:
The candidate's original PDF containing the factual source material for tailored resumes.
_Avoid_: Base CV, source profile

**Job description**:
The pasted posting that defines the target role and its requirements.
_Avoid_: Job prompt

**Tailored resume**:
A separate resume adapted from the master resume for one job description.
_Avoid_: Overwritten original

**Skills section**:
The sole region of the master resume whose skill wording may change for a target job.

**Change plan**:
The proposed additions and removals that the candidate can edit before producing a tailored resume.

**Approved skill**:
A skill the candidate has accepted or added to the change plan, including one absent from the master resume.

**Resume library**:
The candidate's saved master resumes, each independently selectable and replaceable.

**Selected resume**:
The master resume chosen as the source for the current change plan and tailored download.
